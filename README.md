# TikTok Hashtag & Content Analysis Assignment
## Brand Perception: Round Lab vs Competitors (K-skincare brands)

### Project Overview
This small practice analyzes how **Round Lab** is perceived on TikTok compared to key competitors (**Dear, Klairs** and **Torriden**) through branded hashtag performance, content patterns, and audience sentiment.  
The objective is to support **market research and social media strategy** by identifying content drivers, engagement quality, and competitive positioning on TikTok.


### Research Question
**How is Round Lab perceived on TikTok compared to competitors, and what insights can Round Lab (or K-Beauty brands) learn?**

---

**Data Collection Scope**
- Platform: TikTok  
- Period: Last 30 days  
- Brands analyzed: Round Lab, Dear Klairs, Torriden  
- Data sources: TikTok Search, Exolyt  

**Metrics collected**
- Branded hashtag volume (brand mentions)
- Number of videos & total views per hashtag
- Engagement metrics (views, likes, comments, shares) for top 5 videos per hashtag
- Comment-level sentiment (positive / neutral / negative)

### Agenda

#### 1. Competitive Landscape
- Compared brand positioning, hero products, pricing tiers, and communication focus

#### 2. Hashtag Performance Analysis
- Evaluated hashtag volume and growth over time, total views and engagement rate across brands

#### 3. Top-performing Content Analysis
- Analyzed top 5 most-viewed videos per brand: content formats, video length, creator types, and engagement drivers

#### 4. Sentiment Analysis
- Reviewed comment sentiment to understand audience perception and emotional responses toward brands

### Key Findings
- **Round Lab** shows lower awareness but the **highest engagement rate**, indicating strong content relevance despite limited volume.
- **Klairs** leads in reach and visibility but shows lower engagement quality, suggesting content saturation and reliance on paid promotion.
- **Torriden** generates strong interaction and sharing behavior, driven by creator-led and hydration-focused messaging.


### Strategic Insights
- High engagement does not require high content volume; **quality and clarity of positioning matter**.
- Short, informative, and trust-building content performs best across brands.
- Positive sentiment and UGC present an opportunity for Round Lab to scale advocacy-driven growth.


### Some Recommendations
1. Expand creator mix/tiers while preserving Round Lab’s functional, trust-led positioning
2. Diverse content angles while keeping brand core to increase emotional resonance
3. Leverage high-positive sentiment through UGC amplification
